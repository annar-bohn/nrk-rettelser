ctrlz.news

Published corrections from Nordic public broadcasters — collected, classified and searchable.

Public broadcasters correct their own mistakes, but those corrections are published inside the article that carried the error — easy to miss unless you happen to return to it. This site collects them in one place so they can be searched and compared over time.

Each outlet's page documents how its corrections are found and what the method misses. These are independent projects, not affiliated with the broadcasters they cover.
